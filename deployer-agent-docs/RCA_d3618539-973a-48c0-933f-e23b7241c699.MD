## Interpretation (what likely happened)
The 12 records were never persisted to **production**. There is no delete trace. Most likely one of:
1. The data was entered/submitted in the **preview/staging environment** (the preview pod has its OWN separate MongoDB). Initial deploy migrates preview→prod data, but **redeploys do NOT** — so anything entered in preview after go-live never reaches prod. These 12 may be sitting in the preview DB.
2. The public availability-form submissions failed to save (network/validation) and were never written.
3. They were deleted directly (no audit trail for availability/person deletions in `audit_logs`).

I could only query the production DB; I cannot read the preview DB from here, so option (1) cannot be confirmed with my tools — it must be checked from the app/DB-manager side.

- If they were deleted, restore from a backup/export.
- Confirm the intended team name — "Ristoro Verrua" does not exist; it may need to be "Team Ristoro Finale"/"Team Ristori", or a new team to be created.
